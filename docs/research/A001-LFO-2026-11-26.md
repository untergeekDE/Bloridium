# A001 "JMX jun." — LFO A/B and the missing beat (2026-11-26)

Question: why does the converted A001 sound "LFO overdone" (user verdict
2026-11-26: "about half the LFO seems right"), when the A8 calibration
confirmed the LFO->Pitch depth mapping for A001's amounts (raw 70/58)?

## Setup

`scripts/ab_a001_lfo.py`: original Blofeld A001 vs. converted slot 300
(0.6.7), same capture, notes 60 and 72, 8 s each. Modulation analysis =
FFT of the heterodyne pitch trajectory and of the spectral-centroid
trajectory (top peaks in 0.3-20 Hz), so LFO, chorus and beating separate.
Round 2 (`--fx-off`): FX bypassed on both sides (Blofeld: A001 sdata into
the edit buffer with Effect 1/2 = Bypass; Iridium: FX1/FX2 dry) -> what
remains is pure LFO + oscillator detune.

## Results (fx off)

| signal | BF n60 | IR n60 | BF n72 | IR n72 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| centroid-mod peaks | **1.41, 2.13 Hz** | **4.35 Hz** | **2.87, 1.43 Hz** | **4.35 Hz** |
| pitch-mod peaks | 3.65, 1.57, 4.72 | 4.32 (+8.7, +13.1 harmonics) | 2.87, 1.46, 4.25 | 4.37 (+8.7) |

1. **The BF's dominant movement is not the LFO - it is beating.** The
   centroid/pitch modulation *doubles with the octave* (1.41 -> 2.87 Hz):
   Osc1 detune raw 61 (-4.8 cent) and Osc2 raw 67 (+4.8 cent) -> 9.6 cent
   apart -> beat = f0 * (2^(9.6/1200) - 1) = **1.45 Hz at C4, 2.9 Hz at C5**
   - exactly the measured peaks.
2. **The real LFO runs at ~4.3-4.7 Hz on BOTH devices** (note-independent
   peak at 4.25-4.72 Hz BF / 4.32-4.37 Hz IR). The LfoNSpeed mapping
   (norm(raw), A001 raw 58 -> 0.457) is fine for this sound.
3. **The Iridium lost the beat** (no peak at 1.45/2.9 Hz at all). With FX on,
   the IR pitch/brightness modulation is dominated by the LFO at 4.35 Hz -
   perceived as "too much LFO". Halving the matrix amount treats the symptom.
4. **LFO -> F1 Cutoff (raw 103) modulates the IR brightness by 9.6 dB at
   4.35 Hz; the BF shows NO LFO component in the centroid.** The cutoff-mod
   depth mapping is uncalibrated (only Pitch amounts are, per A8).
5. **FX: only types are converted, no parameters.** A001's Chorus (Mix 38,
   P1 28, P2 33) and Clk.Delay become IR default-parameter FX. The BF
   chorus movement (~1.4-2.9 Hz with FX on) has no calibrated counterpart.

## Root cause of the missing beat: template WaveformSpread

The converted qpat has `Osc1/2/3WaveformSpread` = 0.25 / 0.3558 / 0.38 -
these are **template values** (`docs/refs/qpat/template_waveform.qpat`),
identical in every converted sound; the converter never writes them. The
Iridium Waveform oscillator is a 4-voice cluster; Spread detunes those
voices. Every converted osc is therefore a fat unison-ish cluster, while
the poly Blofeld A001 has one voice per osc. The ±4.8-cent inter-osc
detune drowns in the per-osc spread -> no clean beat.

## Consequences (see TODO_FIX / TODO_MEASURE / CALIBRATE.md)

- Template: WaveformSpread -> 0 (or map from Unisono Detune in unison mode
  only); then re-convert and listen. Restores the characteristic beating.
- A9: calibrate LFO -> F1 Cutoff depth (INIT framework, centroid-mod
  BF vs IR over raw amounts).
- FX parameter conversion (chorus rate/depth/mix at minimum) - separate.
- No LfoNSpeed change and no LFO amount dosing for A001 - both were
  measured correct.
